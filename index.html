Why The Prince of Egypt??

The Prince of Egypt, a musical masterpiece

The Prince of Egypt is a 1998 animated musical drama film produced by DreamWorks Animation and directed by Brenda Chapman, Steve Hickner, and Simon Wells. The film is based on the biblical story of Moses and his journey to free the Israelites from slavery in Egypt

The film features an all-star cast of voice actors, including Val Kilmer, Ralph Fiennes, Michelle Pfeiffer, Sandra Bullock, Jeff Goldblum, and Danny Glover. The music for the film was composed by Hans Zimmer, with songs written by Stephen Schwartz.
